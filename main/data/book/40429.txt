Esteemed Ladies, Lords, Adventurers, and Honored Souls of the Realm,

It is with both fond remembrance and mild embarrassment that I, Lord Sebastián Vectorian of House
Vectorian, address you all upon this most unusual season, where the echoes of Valentine's warmth
find themselves fashionably late, arriving hand in hand with the gentle cheer of Eastertide.

Some of you may recall that, not so long ago, I was fortunate - nay, blessed - to receive the aid
of a singularly capable individual. Through their discretion, diligence, and admirable resolve, I
was able to present my beloved Lady Veluvia Barrington with chocolates crafted not merely by
hand, but by heart. The outcome was... most favorable. 

Alas, fate has since conspired to separate me from this invaluable ally. Despite earnest
inquiries and no small amount of searching, I have yet to rediscover the very person whose
talents proved so instrumental. Thus, I find myself compelled to make this public appeal.

To you, my trusted helper of last year, if these words reach your eyes, know this: 
your competence did not go unnoticed, nor your loyalty forgotten. Trust, once earned, is not so
easily misplaced, and I would entrust this next delicate endeavor to no other.^000000

^FFFFFFAnd to the rest of you, should fortune guide the right individual back to my side, I humbly ask
that you permit this message to reach them. For matters of affection, timing is important...
though, as I have recently learned, sincerity may yet triumph even when fashionably late.

Should the one I seek step forward once more, I promise an undertaking worthy of both skill and
heart, seasoned with a hint of sweetness, a touch of springtime cheer, and my personal gratitude.

Until then, may your days be bright, your chocolates plentiful, and your hearts ever open.

With the highest regard,
Lord Sebastián Vectorian

Post Scriptum: Chibi Cupid is naturally ready for you to be of service again.
